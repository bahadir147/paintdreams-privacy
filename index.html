English

Paint Dreams is a children's coloring and drawing game provided by Synverse Games. The game contains advertising through Google AdMob and uses Google Analytics for Firebase. These services process information even when a player does not provide a name or email address.

1. Information and purposes

Advertising — Google AdMob

AdMob processes technical and advertising information to deliver and measure ads, maintain service performance, and detect invalid traffic or fraud. This can include an IP address and an approximate location derived from it, device and app information, ad views and interactions, diagnostics, and device or app identifiers. Availability of identifiers depends on the device, permissions and applicable SDK privacy settings.

Usage measurement — Firebase Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is used and to improve it. Information includes app-instance identifiers, app opens and sessions, engagement duration, app version, device model, operating system, language and general geographic information. Google processes this information to provide usage reports. An app-instance identifier is not the same as a player's name.

Support correspondence

If a parent or guardian contacts us, we receive the email address and information included in the message. We use it to respond to the request. Please do not send a child's name, contact details, photographs or other unnecessary personal information.

2. Children and advertising

The game is intended for children. Children's information must not be used for interest-based advertising, cross-app behavioral profiling or remarketing. Advertising and measurement in a child-directed game must be limited to purposes permitted for children, including providing the service, contextual advertising, service measurement and security. This policy does not provide consent for behavioral advertising to children.

A parent or guardian can contact us about an advertisement, a privacy concern, or information relating to their child. We will investigate the request and take appropriate action, including deleting information under our control where applicable.

3. Service providers and sharing

Google receives information through AdMob and Firebase Analytics to provide the services described above. We may also disclose information when necessary to meet a legal obligation or protect the security of the game. Google may process information in countries other than the player's country under its applicable privacy terms and safeguards.

Further details from the service provider:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Firebase privacy and security

4. Retention, deletion and choices

We keep support correspondence only for as long as needed to handle the request, resolve disputes or meet applicable legal obligations. Analytics information is governed by the property's retention settings and Google's retention and deletion processes. AdMob information is retained by Google according to its policies and the purposes for which it is processed.

Parents or guardians may request access, correction or deletion of information under our control by emailing bahadirdemirbusiness@gmail.com with the subject “Paint Dreams privacy request”. We may ask for limited information needed to locate relevant records and verify the request. Please do not send identity documents or a child's personal details unless specifically necessary and requested.

Android provides controls to clear an app's local storage and, on supported devices, reset or delete the advertising ID. Clearing storage or uninstalling the game does not automatically erase information already processed by a service provider. Some SDK records are linked to an installation rather than a named person; we will explain any limits on locating or deleting such records and direct you to the relevant provider where needed.

5. Security and this website

Google's SDK services use encrypted connections for transmitting data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and server logs, to deliver and protect the website. We have not added advertising or analytics scripts to this page. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

6. Contact and changes

Privacy contact: Synverse Games — bahadirdemirbusiness@gmail.com.

Depending on applicable law, you may have additional rights to object to or restrict processing, request portability, withdraw consent where processing relies on it, or contact a data protection authority. We will publish changes to this policy on this page and update its effective date.

Firebase Analytics: Oyunun nasıl kullanıldığını anlamak ve oyunu geliştirmek için uygulama örneği tanımlayıcıları, uygulama açılışları ve oturumlar, etkileşim süresi, uygulama sürümü, cihaz modeli, işletim sistemi, dil ve genel coğrafi bilgiler işlenir.
